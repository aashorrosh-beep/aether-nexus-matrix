MODULE 1: INFANT CPR (UNDER AGE 1)

• Check responsiveness (tap heel) & call 911.
• Place 2 fingers in center of chest just below nipple line.
• Push 1.5 inches deep at 100–120 beats/min (30 compressions : 2 gentle puffs covering nose & mouth).

MODULE 2: CHOKING RESCUE (5-AND-5 PROTOCOL)

• Infant: Hold face-down on forearm (head lower than chest), deliver 5 firm back slaps between shoulder blades, turn face-up & give 5 chest thrusts.
• Child/Adult: 5 back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich) above navel.

MODULE 3: ADULT & CHILD HANDS-ONLY CPR

• Place heel of hand in center of chest, interlock fingers.
• Push hard & fast (2 inches deep, 100–120 BPM) without stopping until EMS or an AED arrives.
