👶 What to Expect: New Life, Safe Nursery & Free Infant CPR Vault

TRIMESTER ROADMAP • HOSPITAL GO-BAG CHECKLIST • FIRST 30 DAYS SAFE SLEEP • FREE INFANT CPR

1. What to Expect When Expecting: Trimester-by-Trimester & Hospital Go-Bag Checklist

1st, 2nd & 3rd Trimester Roadmap: Trimester 1 (Weeks 1-12): Focus on gentle hydration, prenatal vitamins with methylated folate, choline, and rest. Trimester 2 (Weeks 13-27): Anatomy ultrasound (18-22 wks), nursery setup, and selecting a pediatrician. Trimester 3 (Weeks 28-40): Daily kick counts, Group B Strep screening (36 wks), installing the rear-facing infant car seat, and packing your Hospital Go-Bag.

The Complete Hospital Coming-Home Checklist: 1) Two organic zipper newborn coming-home sleepers (easy diaper access without irritating the umbilical cord) + velcro swaddles. 2) Rear-facing infant car seat (base pre-installed). 3) Postpartum comfort kit for mom (peri bottle, witch hazel pads, nursing comfort wear, electrolyte packets, and a 10-foot phone charger).

2. First 30 Days Home: Safe Sleep (ABC Rule), Feeding Cues & Postpartum Support

AAP Safe Sleep Protocol (The ABCs): Babies sleep safest Alone, on their Back, in a bare Crib or bassinet (firm flat mattress with a fitted sheet only—use wearable sleep sacks or swaddles instead of loose blankets).

Newborn Feeding & Diaper Tracker: Newborns typically feed 8-12 times per 24 hours. By Day 5+, 6 or more wet diapers per day shows strong hydration. 24/7 Free Support for New Parents: National Maternal Mental Health Hotline: 1-833-TLC-MAMA (1-833-852-6262).

3. Free Infant CPR, Choking Response & Free Parenting Classes

Emergency Conscious Choking Infant (<1 Year Old): Hold baby face-down along your forearm with head lower than chest (supporting jaw). Give 5 firm back slaps between the shoulder blades. Turn baby face-up and give 5 two-finger chest thrusts in the center of the breastbone just below the nipple line. Repeat until clear.

Unresponsive Infant CPR (Call 911 Immediately): 30 gentle chest compressions (1.5 inches deep at 100-120/min using 2 fingers) followed by 2 gentle puffs of air. Free Local & Online Classes: Harris County ESD 11 Free 1-Hour Infant CPR & Safe Sleep Class, Community Hospital Parenting Classes, and American Red Cross Online Infant CPR.
